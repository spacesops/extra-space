What should you do with extra space?

A short list of options. Leave it, use it, share it, or drop it.

Options Examples Try

01 / Options

Pick one.

Most leftover space already has a job. If it is truly extra, these are the usual next steps.

Leave it

Keep it empty on purpose.

Give it one job

Sleep, work, store — not all three.

Share it

A guest, a neighbor, a teammate.

Convert it

Add a wall, a block, or a folder.

Get rid of it

Sell, delete, or decline.
